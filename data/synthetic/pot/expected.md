# Six-person Pot trip: worked by hand, before any code ran

Asha holds the money. All amounts are in rupees. Asha asks each of the other five for 5,000.

## Who paid in

| Person | Claim | What happens | Counts |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Rohan | 5,000, 28 Sep, from his statement | Found on Asha's side, confirmed | 5,000 |
| Dev | 5,000, 3 Oct, from his statement | Found, confirmed | 5,000 |
| Farhan | 5,000, 5 Oct, typed, no reference | Found, confirmed | 5,000 |
| Esha | 5,000, 8 Oct, typed, no statement, last four shown | Found, confirmed | 5,000 |
| Meera | 5,000, 7 Oct, from her statement | Failed and reversed. Asha finds nothing and rejects it. Meera's agent finds the reversal, and Meera withdraws it | 0 |
| Meera | 3,000, 10 Oct, from her statement | Found, confirmed, corrected out, corrected back in | 3,000 |

Collected: **23,000**. An unrelated 5,000 from `NEHACANARY K` on Asha's statement must never count or appear.

## What was spent, split among all six

| Paid by | What | Total | Each |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Asha | Villa | 24,000 | 4,000 |
| Asha | Dinner | 9,000 | 1,500 |
| Dev | Groceries | 3,600 | 600 |

Total 36,600, so 6,100 each. Personal lines (Netflix, Spotify, pharmacy, Myntra) never count.

## Before settling

Net = what you spent + what you paid in − your share. Asha also subtracts what she received.

| Person | Spent | Paid in | Received | Share | **Net** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Asha | 33,000 | | 23,000 | 6,100 | **+3,900** |
| Rohan | | 5,000 | | 6,100 | **−1,100** |
| Meera | | 3,000 | | 6,100 | **−3,100** |
| Dev | 3,600 | 5,000 | | 6,100 | **+2,500** |
| Esha | | 5,000 | | 6,100 | **−1,100** |
| Farhan | | 5,000 | | 6,100 | **−1,100** |

The nets sum to zero. Pot position: 23,000 − 33,000 = **−10,000**, so Asha has put in 10,000 of her own, of which 6,100 is her share.

While Meera's 3,000 is corrected out: Asha **+6,900**, Meera **−6,100**, everyone else unchanged.

## Settling, through Asha

| From | To | Amount | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Rohan | Asha | 1,100 | Asha |
| Esha | Asha | 1,100 | Asha |
| Farhan | Asha | 1,100 | Asha |
| Meera | Asha | 3,100 | Asha |
| Asha | Dev | 2,500 | Dev |

Everyone ends at zero. Final position: 23,000 + 6,400 − 33,000 − 2,500 = **−6,100**, exactly Asha's own share.
